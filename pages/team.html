Team Collaboration

Invite your team, assign findings, and get a consolidated view of cloud costs across all members.

ℹ️
Team features are available on the Team plan. Analyses and finding assignment are available on all paid plans.

Creating a team

  1. Go to Account → Team
  2. Click Create Team and enter a team name
  3. Your team workspace is created immediately

Inviting members

  1. Go to Account → Team
  2. Enter a colleague email address in the Invite a team member field
  3. Select a role: Member, Admin, or Viewer
  4. Click Send Invite

The invitee receives an email with a link to join your team. They need a Rackwyse account to accept.

Team roles

RoleCan do
OwnerFull access, billing, invite/remove members, team settings
AdminInvite/remove members, manage all analyses and findings
MemberUpload bills, run analyses, manage own findings
ViewerView analyses and findings, read-only

Team cost overview

Team owners and admins see a Team cost overview card at the top of the Team tab showing:

  • Total team cloud spend across all members
  • Total savings identified
  • Total open issues
  • Per-member breakdown with spend, savings found, and open issue count

Weekly team digest

Every Monday at 09:00 UTC, the team owner receives a Weekly Team Digest email summarising:

  • Total team spend this month
  • Total savings identified
  • Open issues across the team
  • Per-member spend and savings table

Assigning findings

Any team member can assign a finding to a colleague:

  1. Expand the finding on the dashboard
  2. Click Assign
  3. Enter the team member email and set a due date
  4. Change status to In Progress
