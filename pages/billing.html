💡
Analysis count resets monthly
Your analysis count resets on the 1st of each month. Unused analyses do not roll over to the next month.

Upgrading your plan

1
Go to Account Settings
Click your avatar or name in the top right and select Account, or go to app.rackwyse.com/account.
2
Click Billing & Plan
In the account sub-navigation, click Billing & Plan. You will see your current plan and the available upgrade options.
3
Select a plan
Click the Upgrade button on the plan you want. You will be taken to a secure Dodo Payments checkout page.
4
Complete payment
Enter your card details and complete the payment. Your plan upgrades immediately. You will be redirected to a confirmation page.

Payment history & receipts

All payments are recorded in your account and can be downloaded as PDF receipts for expense reporting and accounting.

1
Go to Billing & Plan
Navigate to Account → Billing & Plan.
2
Find Payment History
Scroll down to the Payment History section. All past payments are listed with the date, amount, plan, and payment method.
3
Download a receipt
Click the ↓ Receipt button next to any payment. A receipt page opens with your full payment details. Click Download PDF to save it.

Receipts include: receipt number, payment date, plan name, amount, payment method, and your account email. They are formatted for expense submission.

Cancelling your subscription

To cancel your subscription, go to Account → Billing & Plan and click Manage / Cancel Subscription. Contact support@rackwyse.com to process your cancellation.

ℹ️
Access continues until period end
When you cancel, you retain full access to your current plan until the end of your billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free plan (1 analysis/month).

Accepted payment methods

Rackwyse accepts payment via Dodo Payments, which supports Visa, Mastercard, and American Express cards in 150+ countries. We are working on adding PayPal as an additional option.
