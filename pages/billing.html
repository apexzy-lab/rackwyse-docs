Plans & Billing

Rackwyse offers monthly and annual plans for individuals and teams.

Current plans

PlanAnalyses/monthCloud connectionsPrice
Free Trial11$0 (14 days)

Annual plans save 20% vs monthly. All paid plans include AWS, GCP & Azure support, all 99 detection rules, PDF export, shareable reports, and email support.

Free tier limits

Free and trial accounts see 5 full findings per analysis with complete details including CLI fix commands. Additional findings are shown as locked cards with the savings amount visible. The locked savings total is shown in an upgrade banner.

Upgrading your plan

  1. Go to Account → Billing & Plan
  2. Click Upgrade next to the plan you want
  3. Complete payment via Dodo Payments (credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay)
  4. Your plan upgrades immediately

Payment processor

Rackwyse processes payments via Dodo Payments, a Merchant of Record that handles global payment processing, tax compliance, and refunds.

Cancelling

Go to Account → Billing & Plan → Manage Subscription. You can cancel at any time. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.

Refunds

If you are not satisfied within the first 7 days of a paid subscription, contact support@rackwyse.com for a full refund.

Annual billing

Annual plans are charged upfront for the full year at a 20% discount. Toggle between monthly and annual pricing on the pricing page.
