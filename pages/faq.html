Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Rackwyse. Can't find what you need? Email support@rackwyse.com.

General

How does Rackwyse analyse my bill?
Rackwyse uses an AI engine trained on cloud billing best practices and known waste patterns. When you upload a billing CSV, the engine parses your line items, identifies anomalies and inefficiencies, and cross-references them against optimal usage benchmarks for each service type. Results are generated in under 60 seconds.
Do I need to give Rackwyse access to my cloud account?
No. You can use Rackwyse entirely by uploading billing export CSV files — no cloud credentials, API keys, or access permissions needed. Cloud Connections (optional, for Pro/Team plans) allow you to connect an account for streamlined uploads, but this is never required.
Can Rackwyse make changes to my cloud infrastructure?
No. Rackwyse is a read-only analysis tool. It never modifies, deletes, starts, or stops any cloud resources. Even when using Cloud Connections, the permissions granted are strictly read-only billing data access. All changes to your infrastructure must be made by you or your team directly in the cloud console.

Analysis Limits

How many analyses can I run?
Analysis limits depend on your plan: Free (1/month), Starter (3/month), Pro (20/month), Team (100/month). Your analysis count resets on the 1st of each month. You can see your current usage in the dashboard or in Account → Billing & Plan.
What happens when I reach my limit?
When you reach your monthly analysis limit, you will see an upgrade prompt when you try to upload a new bill. You can upgrade your plan immediately to continue analysing, or wait for your limit to reset on the 1st of next month.
Do unused analyses roll over to next month?
No. Analysis limits reset on the 1st of each month. Unused analyses do not carry over.

File Formats

What file types does Rackwyse accept?
Rackwyse accepts CSV files (.csv) from AWS, GCP, and Azure billing exports. Maximum file size is 50MB. If your export is compressed (.csv.gz), decompress it before uploading.
What billing exports are supported?
AWS: Bills CSV (from Billing Console), Cost & Usage Report (CUR). GCP: Billing Reports CSV (from Cloud Console). Azure: Usage Details CSV (from Cost Management). Other formats may work but are not officially supported.
Can I upload multiple months at once?
Each upload is one analysis. To compare multiple months, upload each month separately and use the Savings Tracker comparison feature to see how your spending has changed over time.

Data Privacy & Security

Is my billing data secure?
Yes. All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Billing files are processed in memory and not permanently stored in raw form. Analysis results (findings and savings estimates) are stored securely in our database. We do not share your billing data with third parties.
Does Rackwyse store my cloud credentials?
When using Cloud Connections, Rackwyse stores only the IAM Role ARN (for AWS) or OAuth tokens (for GCP/Azure). We do not store access keys, passwords, or secret keys. IAM roles use cross-account trust with an External ID for additional security. You can revoke access at any time by deleting the IAM role or revoking the OAuth token.
Can I delete my data?
Yes. To delete your account and all associated data, email support@rackwyse.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days in accordance with GDPR and applicable data protection laws.

Billing

When am I charged?
You are charged immediately when you subscribe to a paid plan. Subsequent charges occur on the same date each month (your billing anniversary). If your card fails, you will receive an email notification and have a grace period to update your payment method.
Can I get a refund?
If you are not satisfied with Rackwyse within the first 7 days of your paid subscription, contact support@rackwyse.com for a full refund. After 7 days, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis.
Do you offer annual plans?
Yes. Annual plans are available at a discount compared to monthly billing. Contact support@rackwyse.com or check the pricing page for current annual pricing.
