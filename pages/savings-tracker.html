Savings Tracker

The Savings Tracker shows your most recent findings, lets you mark issues as fixed, and tracks your total confirmed savings over time.

Accessing the Savings Tracker

Click Savings Tracker in the left sidebar. The tracker shows findings from your most recent analysis that has findings — it automatically skips empty auto-sync analyses.

What you see

  • Spend Trend chart — monthly spend and savings potential over time
  • Bill History — all past analyses with spend and finding counts
  • Fix Tracker — all findings with their current status
  • Progress bar — percentage of findings resolved and confirmed savings

Marking findings as fixed

Click the circle on the left of any finding to cycle through statuses:

StatusMeaning
⚪ OpenFinding identified, not yet actioned
🔵 In ProgressWork started, not yet complete
✅ FixedIssue resolved — savings confirmed and added to total
— DismissedFinding acknowledged but not fixing (does not count toward savings)

Confirmed savings

When you mark a finding as Fixed, its estimated monthly savings are added to your Confirmed Saved total. This is the real ROI from using Rackwyse.

Filtering findings

Use the tabs at the top of the Fix Tracker to filter by status: All, Open, In Progress, Fixed, Dismissed. This helps focus on what still needs attention.

Month-over-month comparison

The Bill History panel on the right shows each past analysis with its spend, finding count, and month-over-month delta. Green deltas mean spend decreased. Red means spend increased.
