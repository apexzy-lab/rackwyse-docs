Savings Tracker

Track which findings you have implemented and monitor your total cumulative savings across all analyses.

What is the Savings Tracker?

The Savings Tracker is a dedicated view at app.rackwyse.com/tracker that gives you a running total of your implemented savings. As you fix issues identified in your reports, mark them as resolved and Rackwyse tracks your progress.

The tracker shows:

  • Total potential savings — the sum of all estimated savings across all open findings
  • Implemented savings — the sum of savings from findings you have marked as fixed
  • Savings implemented this month — monthly progress view
  • All findings — a complete list of findings from all analyses, with their status

Marking findings as fixed

1
Open the Savings Tracker
Click Savings Tracker in the left sidebar of your dashboard, or go directly to app.rackwyse.com/tracker.
2
Find the finding
Browse the findings list. You can filter by status (open, in progress, fixed) and sort by savings amount or severity.
3
Update the status
Click the status button next to a finding and select the new status. Open means not yet addressed. In Progress means you are working on it. Fixed means the issue has been resolved.
4
Watch your savings accumulate
When you mark a finding as Fixed, its estimated savings are added to your total implemented savings. The KPI cards at the top of the tracker update immediately.

Comparing analyses

The Savings Tracker includes a comparison banner when you have more than one analysis. This shows you how your spending has changed between the current and previous bill — whether your waste has increased, decreased, or stayed flat.

💡
Upload monthly for the best insights
Upload a new bill every month to track your progress over time. The comparison view becomes more useful as you build up a history of analyses, showing you whether your optimisation efforts are having a real effect.

Best practices

Work through findings in severity order

Start with Critical and High severity findings — they have the largest savings potential. Once those are addressed, work through Medium and Low findings.

Use In Progress for team coordination

When you are waiting for another team member to implement a fix, set the finding to In Progress. This signals that the work is underway without claiming the savings yet.

Verify on your next bill

After marking a finding as Fixed, upload your next month's bill to verify the change actually reduced your costs. If the finding reappears in the new analysis, the fix may not have been complete.

Do not mark as fixed without implementing

Only mark findings as Fixed when you have actually implemented the change. The saved total is most useful when it reflects real cost reductions, not aspirational ones.
