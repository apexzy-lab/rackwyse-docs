Cloud Connections

Connect your cloud accounts to Rackwyse for automatic monthly billing syncs. All connections are read-only — Rackwyse never modifies your infrastructure.

ℹ️
Connections vs manual uploads: You do not need to connect a cloud account to use Rackwyse. You can always upload billing CSVs manually. Cloud connections enable automatic monthly syncs and utilization-based findings (idle instances, over-provisioned resources).

☁️ AWS — IAM Role

Rackwyse uses an IAM Role with read-only billing and CloudWatch permissions. No access keys are stored — Rackwyse assumes your role using STS with a unique External ID.

Required IAM policies

  • AWSCostExplorerReadOnlyAccess — read billing and cost data
  • CloudWatchReadOnlyAccess — enables utilization-based findings (idle/oversized EC2)

Setup steps

1

Open Connections in Rackwyse

Click Connections in the sidebar. Note the External ID shown — you will need it below.

2

Create an IAM Role in AWS

Go to AWS IAM Console → Roles → Create Role. Select Another AWS account, enter the Rackwyse Account ID shown on the Connections page, check Require external ID, and paste the External ID from step 1.

3

Attach required policies

On the Permissions page, attach AWSCostExplorerReadOnlyAccess and CloudWatchReadOnlyAccess.

4

Name and create the role

Name the role RackwyseReadOnly and create it. Copy the Role ARN from the summary page.

5

Paste the ARN in Rackwyse

Back in the Rackwyse Connections page, paste the Role ARN and click Connect. Rackwyse verifies the connection immediately.

💡
AWS connections auto-sync on the day of the month you configure (default: 1st). You can also click Sync Now at any time for an immediate analysis.

🔵 GCP — OAuth + BigQuery

GCP connections use Google OAuth with read-only billing access. After connecting, Rackwyse queries your Cloud Billing BigQuery export for detailed cost data.

Prerequisites

  • Enable Cloud Billing API in your GCP project
  • Enable BigQuery billing export in GCP Console → Billing → Billing export → BigQuery export
  • Wait 24–48 hours for the first billing data to appear in BigQuery after enabling export

Setup steps

1

Click "Connect with Google"

On the Connections page, click Connect with Google and complete the OAuth flow. Grant read-only billing access.

2

Enter your BigQuery details

After OAuth, a setup modal appears asking for:
• GCP Project ID — the project containing your billing export (e.g. my-company-project)
• BigQuery Dataset Name — the dataset where billing export is configured (e.g. billing_export)
• Region — where your BigQuery dataset is located (US, EU, etc.)

3

Save and enable sync

Click Save & Enable Sync. Rackwyse tests the BigQuery connection immediately. If the table has no data yet, the connection stays active and will sync automatically once data populates.

⚠️
BigQuery billing export only captures usage going forward from the date you enable it. Historical data is not backfilled. Allow 24–48 hours for the first data to appear.

🔷 Azure — OAuth

Azure connections use Microsoft OAuth with Cost Management Reader role on your subscription.

Prerequisites

  • Your Azure account must have a subscription with Cost Management Reader role
  • The Rackwyse app must be registered in Azure AD as multi-tenant

Setup steps

1

Click "Connect with Microsoft"

On the Connections page, click Connect with Microsoft. Sign in with your Azure account and grant the requested read-only permissions.

2

Connection verified

Rackwyse automatically detects your subscription and begins the first sync. You will be redirected to the Connections page showing your subscription ID and sync status.

Auto-sync settings

Each connection has an Auto-sync toggle and a configurable sync day (1st–28th of the month). When enabled, Rackwyse automatically pulls your latest billing data on the configured day and runs a new analysis. Anomaly alerts fire automatically if spend increases significantly.

Disconnecting

To disconnect a cloud account, click Disconnect on the Connections page. For AWS, also delete the IAM role in your AWS console to fully revoke access. For GCP/Azure, revoke the OAuth token in your Google/Microsoft account settings.
