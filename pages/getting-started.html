Getting Started

Create your account, connect your cloud, and run your first analysis in under 5 minutes.

1. Create your account

Go to app.rackwyse.com/register and fill in your name, work email, and a password (minimum 8 characters).

ℹ️
Rackwyse requires a work email address. Free providers like Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook are not accepted.

2. Verify your email

Check your inbox for a verification email from noreply@rackwyse.com. Click the link to activate your account. The link expires after 24 hours.

⚠️
Check your spam folder if you do not receive the email within 2 minutes.

3. Log in and connect your cloud

Once approved, go to app.rackwyse.com and log in. Your dashboard shows a 3-step onboarding card.

The recommended first step is to connect your cloud account via IAM Role (AWS) or OAuth (GCP/Azure). This takes 2 minutes and enables automatic monthly syncs. You can also upload a billing CSV manually if you prefer.

🔗
Connect your cloud account
IAM Role for AWS, OAuth for GCP/Azure — recommended
📤
Upload a billing CSV
Manual upload — no account connection required

4. Review your findings

After your first analysis completes, you will see a report with findings sorted by estimated savings. The free tier shows 5 full findings — enough to demonstrate real value. Upgrade to unlock all findings.

Each finding includes:

  • Severity level (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
  • Estimated monthly savings
  • Plain-English description of the waste
  • Exact CLI command to fix it
  • Console steps walkthrough
  • Official documentation link

5. Track your savings

As you fix issues, mark them as resolved in the Savings Tracker. Your confirmed savings accumulate across all analyses.

💡
Your free trial includes full access for 14 days. No credit card required to start.
