Getting Started

Set up your account

Create your Rackwyse account, verify your email, and run your first cloud cost analysis in under 5 minutes.

1. Create your account

Go to app.rackwyse.com/register and fill in your name, work email address, and a password (minimum 8 characters).

ℹ️
Work email required
Rackwyse requires a work email address (e.g. yourname@company.com). Free email providers like Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook are not accepted. This ensures we can verify you are associated with the organisation whose cloud bills you are analysing.

2. Verify your email

After registering, check your inbox for a verification email from noreply@rackwyse.com. Click the verification link to activate your account.

⚠️
Check your spam folder
Verification emails occasionally land in spam. If you do not see the email within 2 minutes, check your spam/junk folder. You can also request a new verification email from the login page.

The verification link expires after 24 hours. If your link has expired, go to the login page and click "Resend verification email."

3. Account approval

Rackwyse uses an approval-based signup process to ensure quality access and prevent abuse. After verifying your email, your account will be reviewed and approved by the Rackwyse team — typically within a few hours during business hours.

You will receive an email once your account is approved. Until then, you will see a message explaining that your account is pending approval when you try to log in.

💡
Need faster approval?
Email support@rackwyse.com with your registered email address and we will expedite your approval.

4. Log in for the first time

Once approved, go to app.rackwyse.com and log in with your email and password. You will land on the dashboard with an onboarding guide.

5. The onboarding guide

Your first login shows a 3-step onboarding card on the dashboard:

1
Create your account — done
This step is pre-completed. Your account is verified and ready.
2
Upload your first cloud bill
Download a billing export from your AWS, GCP, or Azure console and upload it. See the Uploading Bills guide for step-by-step instructions for each provider.
3
Review your savings report
Once your bill is analysed, click into your report to see findings, estimated savings, and specific fix instructions.
💡
Your first analysis is free
Every account includes one free analysis. No credit card required to get started. You will only be prompted to upgrade if you want to run additional analyses.
