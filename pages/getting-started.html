Getting Started

Create your account, connect your cloud, and run your first analysis in under 5 minutes.

1. Create your account

Go to app.rackwyse.com/register and fill in your name, work email, and a password (minimum 8 characters).

ℹ️
Rackwyse requires a work email address. Free providers like Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook are not accepted.

2. Verify your email

Check your inbox for a verification email from noreply@rackwyse.com. Click the link to activate your account. The link expires after 24 hours.

⚠️
Check your spam folder if you do not receive the email within 2 minutes. You can request a new verification email from the login page.

3. Connect your cloud or upload a bill

After login, your dashboard shows a 3-step onboarding card. The recommended first step is to connect your cloud account — this enables automatic monthly syncs and utilization-based findings.

🔗
Connect your cloud
IAM Role for AWS, OAuth for GCP/Azure — recommended
📤
Upload a billing CSV
Manual upload — no cloud connection needed

4. Review your findings

After your first analysis, you will see findings sorted by estimated savings. The free tier shows 5 full findings — enough to demonstrate real value. Upgrade to unlock all findings.

Each finding includes severity level, estimated monthly savings, plain-English description, CLI fix command, console steps, and official documentation link.

5. Track your savings

As you fix issues, mark them resolved in the Savings Tracker. Confirmed savings accumulate across all analyses.

💡
Your free trial includes full access for 14 days. No credit card required to start.
