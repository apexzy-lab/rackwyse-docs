What is Rackwyse?

Rackwyse is a cloud cost optimisation platform that analyses your AWS, GCP, and Azure billing data to find exactly where you are overspending. Connect your cloud account via read-only IAM or OAuth, or upload a billing CSV — within 60 seconds you get a prioritised list of findings, each with a specific CLI fix command and estimated monthly savings.

Most users find $500–$2,000/month in waste on their first analysis.

How it works

1

Sign up and verify your email

Create your account at app.rackwyse.com. Verify your email to activate.

2

Connect your cloud account or upload a bill

Connect via IAM Role (AWS) or OAuth (GCP/Azure) for automatic monthly syncs, or upload a billing CSV directly. No write permissions ever required.

3

Review your findings

Your report shows every waste pattern found across 99 detection rules, sorted by severity and estimated savings. Each finding includes a plain-English description, exact CLI command, and console steps.

4

Fix and track savings

Copy the CLI command, assign the finding to a team member, mark it done. Confirmed savings accumulate in the Savings Tracker.

Supported cloud providers

☁️ Amazon Web Services (AWS) — 40 detection rules
🔵 Google Cloud Platform (GCP) — 31 detection rules
🔷 Microsoft Azure — 25 detection rules

Multi-cloud analysis is supported. The dashboard and Savings Tracker aggregate findings across all providers.
