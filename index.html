What is Rackwyse?

Rackwyse is a cloud cost optimisation platform that analyses your AWS, GCP, and Azure billing data to find exactly where you are overspending. Connect via read-only IAM or OAuth, or upload a billing CSV — within 60 seconds you get a prioritised list of findings, each with a specific CLI fix command and estimated monthly savings.

Most users find $500–$2,000/month in waste on their first analysis. Rackwyse currently has 99 detection rules across AWS (40), GCP (31), and Azure (25).

How it works

1

Create your account

Sign up at app.rackwyse.com. Verify your email to activate. 14-day free trial included.

2

Connect your cloud or upload a bill

Connect via IAM Role (AWS) or OAuth (GCP/Azure) for automatic monthly syncs — or upload a billing CSV directly. No write permissions ever required.

3

Review your findings

Your report shows every waste pattern found, sorted by estimated savings. Each finding includes a plain-English description, CLI fix command, and console steps.

4

Fix and track savings

Copy the CLI command, assign findings to team members, mark them done. Confirmed savings accumulate in the Savings Tracker.
