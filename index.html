What is Rackwyse?

Rackwyse is a cloud cost optimisation platform that analyses your AWS, GCP, and Azure billing data to find exactly where you are overspending. Upload your billing export, and within 60 seconds you get a prioritised list of findings — idle resources, oversized instances, unused reservations, misconfigured services — each with a specific fix and an estimated monthly savings.

Most users find $500–$2,000/month in waste on their first analysis. Rackwyse does not require any cloud credentials or special permissions — you export a billing file from your cloud console and upload it directly.

How it works

1
Sign up and verify your email
Create your account at app.rackwyse.com. You will receive a verification email. Click the link to activate your account. Rackwyse uses an approval process — you may receive an email once your account is approved.
2
Export your billing data
Download a billing export from your AWS, GCP, or Azure console. This is a CSV file containing your cost data. No API keys or cloud access permissions are needed — just the export file.
3
Upload and analyse
Click the Upload Bill button on your dashboard, select your CSV file, and Rackwyse analyses it in under 60 seconds. The AI engine cross-references your usage patterns against best practices and known waste patterns.
4
Review your findings
Your report shows every waste pattern found, sorted by severity and estimated savings. Each finding includes a plain-English description and specific steps to fix it.
5
Track your savings
As you fix issues, mark them as resolved in the Savings Tracker. Your cumulative savings are tracked across all analyses so you always know your total ROI from Rackwyse.

Supported cloud providers

☁️ Amazon Web Services (AWS)
🔵 Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
🔷 Microsoft Azure

Multi-cloud analysis is supported — you can upload bills from different providers and Rackwyse analyses them independently. The Savings Tracker aggregates findings across all providers.
